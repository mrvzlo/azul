Mosaic Ladder

Choose how many opponents to play with.

New to the game?
  1. On your turn, take all tiles of one color from a factory, or take that color from the center
  2. Place the tiles in a pattern line, any extras go to the penalty row
  3. When the factories and center are empty, filled lines move one tile to the matching wall space and gain score for adjacent tiles count
  4. The game ends when a player completes a row
  5. Full row, full column and full color give bonus score
